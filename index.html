Molecular Quantum Emitters for Quantum Key Distribution

Author: Ghulam Murtaza

Institution: Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II

Department: Dipartimento di Fisica

Degree Program: PhD in Quantum Technologies (Tecnologie Quantistiche, Cycle 35)

Advisors / Tutors: Costanza Toninelli, Francesco Tafuri

Date of Defense: 10 June 2023

Abstract

Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) allows the distribution of cryptographic keys between multiple users in an information-theoretic secure way, exploiting principles of quantum physics. This thesis focuses on the implementation and characterization of molecular quantum emitters, investigating single-photon emission, polarization modulation, and Hong-Ou-Mandel interference for robust quantum communication and QKD protocols.
